# Seated villagers (current)

`villagers-seated.glb` contains Riley and Sam, adapted from the free Innerscene
"Riley and Sam - 3D Person, Two people at a table" model.

- Publisher/source: https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/riley-and-sam-3d-person-two-people-at-a-table-1833f30f
- Original basis: MakeHuman/MPFB CC0 body, skin, clothing and hair assets, adapted by Innerscene.
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Downloaded: 2026-10-20.
- Changes: removed the original table/chairs, extracted both complete seated figures,
  simplified geometry with locked boundaries, compacted vertex buffers, capped
  color textures at 1024 px and normal maps at 512 px, compressed opaque color maps
  to JPEG, and normalized orientation. Approximately 25,174 / 22,617 triangles.
- Preparation: `scripts/prepare-villager-models.cjs` (takes the source GLB as an argument).
- Runtime: scaled to the game table and fitted with locally generated wooden chairs.
  No old head or procedural body is placed underneath.
- 2026-10-21 roster: the original two figures plus ten locally authored derivatives
  of these same CC0 assets, generated by `src/client/villager-variants.mjs` and
  described in `src/client/villager-roster.mjs`. Derivatives adjust head/build
  proportions, hair shape/color and shirts, and add locally generated glasses,
  moustaches, a straw hat, hair bun and headscarves. They are variations of the two
  base figures, not ten separately sourced scans. Shared skin/normal maps and
  bounded 512 px recolor textures avoid downloading twelve full character files.
  Living/dead instances use separate materials; eliminated figures are grey.

## Seated character revision

`villager-head-seated.glb` is derived from the same Lee Perry-Smith scan under CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith . Modified by cropping the shoulder geometry below the neck, rescaling, and rotating toward the table. Original license: `LeePerrySmith_License.txt`. The seated clothed body and chair are generated by this project; the scanned shoulder bust is not stacked on top of that body.
